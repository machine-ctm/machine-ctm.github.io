03Sách miễn phí · Free online books

Free books online Sách miễn phí trực tuyến

Genuinely free, open-access machining books you can read online or download as PDF — hosted on the Internet Archive and other free library sources; no institution login required (a free Internet Archive account may be needed to borrow some editions).

OPEN ACCESS

CNC Programming Handbook — Peter Smid

The de facto standard reference for CNC programming: G/M-code, setup, and practical examples. Full PDF available to read or download free. Sách tham khảo chuẩn về lập trình CNC; tải PDF miễn phí.

 PUBLIC DOMAIN

Modern Machine-Shop Practice (Vol. I & II) — Joshua Rose

Classic public-domain machinist's manual: tools, lathes, planers, drilling and shop practice. Free to read online (EPUB / HTML / MOBI). Sách thuộc phạm vi công cộng; đọc miễn phí.

 FREE PDF

The Use and Care of Twist Drills — Cleveland Twist Drill Co.

Practical handbook on drill geometry, selection, grinding and troubleshooting twist drills. Free PDF. Cẩm nang thực hành về mũi khoan; PDF miễn phí.

 FREE PDF

Die Design Fundamentals — Paquin

Foundational text on die design for stamping and sheet-metal forming. Free PDF. Nền tảng thiết kế khuôn dập; PDF miễn phí.

 FREE PDF

Fundamentals of Machining and Machine Tools — Boothroyd

Classic university text on machining processes and machine tools by Geoffrey Boothroyd. Free PDF (hosted by College Library / referenceglobe.com). Sách giáo trình kinh điển về gia công và máy công cụ; PDF miễn phí.

 FREE PDF

CNC Machining Process Guide — Eagle Group

Practical industry guide to CNC machining processes and best practices, published by Eagle Group Manufacturers. Free PDF. Cẩm nang thực hành quy trình gia công CNC; PDF miễn phí.
04Thư viện & học thuật · Library references

Library & academic references Tài liệu thư viện & học thuật

Curated from the BCIT Machinist LibGuide and academic sources. Some require an institutional login; free alternatives are noted.

BCIT LIBGUIDE

Machinery's Handbook (2024 ed.)

The definitive mechanical-engineering reference. BCIT students access via Knovel; a borrowable scan is also on the Internet Archive (free IA account). BCIT truy cập qua Knovel; bản mượn trên Internet Archive.

 WORLDCAT / BCIT

CNC Trade Secrets — James A. Harvey (2015)

Practical CNC machine-shop know-how: coding, workholding and job analysis. BCIT access via WorldCat. Kinh nghiệm thực tế xưởng CNC; BCIT qua WorldCat.

 RESEARCHGATE

Machining Processes and Machines: Fundamentals, Analysis and Calculations

Academic text on machining processes, machine analysis and calculations — the same title as the 32-page preview hosted above. Request the full book from the authors via ResearchGate, or obtain it from the publisher. Sách học thuật; yêu cầu toàn văn từ tác giả qua ResearchGate.

 BORROW FREE

Machinery's Handbook — Oberg, Jones & Horton (archive scan)

Borrowable historical scan of the classic handbook on the Internet Archive (free account, fixed loan period). Bản quét cũ có thể mượn miễn phí trên Internet Archive.
05Tham chiếu nhanh · Quick reference

G-code & M-code quick reference Tham chiếu G / M

Common Fanuc-style codes. For a full course, see the CnCCookbook G-code course ↗.

CodeMeaning · Ý nghĩa
G00Rapid positioning · Chạy dao nhanh
G01Linear interpolation (cut) · Nội suy thẳng
G02 / G03Circular interp. CW / CCW · Nội suy cung tròn
G17/18/19Plane select XY / ZX / YZ
G20 / G21Inch / Metric units
G28Return to reference point · Về điểm gốc
G40/41/42Cutter comp. off / left / right
G43Tool length offset · Bù trừ chiều dài dao
G54–G59Work coordinate systems
G80Cancel canned cycle · Hủy chu trình
G81 / G83Drilling / deep-drilling cycle
G90 / G91Absolute / Incremental
G94 / G95Feed /min · Feed /rev
CodeMeaning · Ý nghĩa
M00Program stop · Dừng chương trình
M01Optional stop
M03 / M04Spindle CW / CCW · Trục chính thuận/nghịch
M05Spindle stop
M06Tool change · Đổi dao
M08 / M09Coolant on / off · Bơm/nước tắt
M30Program end & reset · Kết thúc
M98 / M99Subprogram call / return
SSpindle speed (RPM)
FFeed rate
TTool number
NSequence (line) number

DISCLAIMER Mã G/M chuẩn Fanuc; thực tế tùy thuộc máy và bộ điều khiển. Luôn kiểm tra tài liệu máy trước khi chạy. Standard Fanuc-style codes; actual behavior depends on the machine and controller. Always verify against your machine manual before running.

06Khoa học nền tảng · Foundation science

Foundation Science Khoa học nền tảng

Thirteen genuinely free science books — maths puzzles, Victorian physics, candle chemistry and wild ideas about the natural world — also featured on the Foundation page ↩. Open any one in the in-browser Reader, or download it. Bản tiếng Việt: /vi/books/#foundation-science ▸

01 · PUBLIC DOMAIN · 1884

Flatland

Edwin A. Abbott · A Romance of Many Dimensions

Imagine an entire world where everybody is a flat shape, and being a triangle is a social class. Then a Sphere drops in from above and poor Mr Square's whole universe falls apart. Short, funny, slightly rude about Victorian society — and still the clearest trip into a fourth dimension anyone has written.

Reader ▸ Download · 0.6 MB Source ↩
02 · PUBLIC DOMAIN · 1917

Amusements in Mathematics

Henry Ernest Dudeney · 430 puzzles

A book that opens with a puzzle about a spider and a fly and simply never stops. No homework, no graded exercises — just "can you work it out?" Some answers take thirty seconds, one or two might eat your whole weekend.

Reader ▸ Download · 7.4 MB Source ↩
03 · PROJECT GUTENBERG

Mathematical Recreations and Essays

W. W. Rouse Ball · magic squares, knots, mazes

Magic squares, how to get out of a maze, why a knot is a mathematical object, and the famous puzzle of colouring a map with only four crayons. Written by someone who clearly thought maths was the best party trick available.

Reader ▸ Download · 2.5 MB Source ↩
04 · PUBLIC DOMAIN · VICTORIAN

Scientific Amusements for Young People

John Henry Pepper · the original science show

Pepper ran the most famous science theatre in London and invented a ghost illusion that still carries his name. This is his book of tricks for young readers — except every trick comes with the honest explanation of the physics behind it.

Reader ▸ Download · 6.7 MB Source ↩
05 · PUBLIC DOMAIN

The Story of Electricity for Amateurs and Students

From amber and fur to the telegraph

It begins with someone rubbing a piece of amber with a cloth two thousand years ago and ends with wires crossing oceans. A story about the strangest invisible thing in everyday life, told by people who were still astonished by it.

Reader ▸ Download · 8.3 MB Source ↩
06 · PUBLIC DOMAIN · BIG SCAN

Scientific Amusements

Gaston Tissandier · illustrated, 24.8 MB

A big, beautifully illustrated French book of physics-as-fun: floating soap bubbles, singing flames, magnetic toys, little machines you can picture building. It is the largest file in this section, which is exactly why it lives on Releases instead of the site.

Reader ▸ Download · 24.8 MB Source ↩
07 · PUBLIC DOMAIN · 1861

The Chemical History of a Candle

Michael Faraday · six Christmas lectures for children

Six lectures, one candle, and almost the whole of chemistry: where the wax goes, why the flame is hollow, what the colours mean, how a candle is secretly a little factory, and why breathing and burning are cousins. Still the gold standard for making science feel like a story.

Reader ▸ Download · 3.5 MB Source ↩
08 · PUBLIC DOMAIN · 1896

The Story of a Piece of Coal

Geoffrey Martin · a biography of a lump of coal

A whole book that treats a lump of coal as a character with a life story: ancient forests, pressure, deep time, fire, steam engines, the clothes you are wearing. It is geology and chemistry disguised as an adventure, and it is short.

Reader ▸ Download · 2.5 MB Source ↩
09 · PUBLIC DOMAIN · 1911

Triumphs & Wonders of Modern Chemistry

Geoffrey Martin · a popular account, no exam in sight

Liquid air, artificial diamonds, the mystery of the elements, and the peculiar excitement of a century that had just discovered radioactivity. Written for ordinary readers at the exact moment chemistry started feeling like science fiction.

Reader ▸ Download · 12.8 MB Source ↩
10 · US GOVERNMENT · PUBLIC DOMAIN

DOE Fundamentals Handbook: Chemistry, Vol. 1

U.S. Department of Energy · the serious one

Not a bedtime read — this is the plain, careful reference you reach for when a fun book mentions "atomic structure" or "chemical bonding" and you want the real, checkable version. Free because it was paid for by public money. Keep it on the shelf for later.

Reader ▸ Download · 4.2 MB
11 · PUBLIC DOMAIN · 1879

The Fairy-Land of Science

Arabella B. Buckley · fairy tales without the fairies

"The history of a grain of sand", "the life of a primrose", "a sunbeam's journey" — Buckley wrote real science in the shape of fairy stories, because she thought wonder was a better teacher than memorising. She was right.

Reader ▸ Download · 7.2 MB Source ↩
12 · PUBLIC DOMAIN

The Wonders of Science in Modern Life

Early 20th-century popular science

A time capsule from an era when electricity, flight, radio and the motor car all felt brand new. Half of it is a prediction about the future; the other half is a very earnest attempt to explain the present. Both halves are charming.

Reader ▸ Download · 4.4 MB Source ↩
13 · PUBLIC DOMAIN · 1859

On the Origin of Species

Charles Darwin · the book that changed the question

Not a textbook: an argument, written for general readers, built out of pigeons, barnacles, beetles and islands. Read the first chapter on domestic pigeons and you will see how patiently a huge idea can be assembled out of small, ordinary observations.
