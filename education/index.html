01Lộ trình nghề · Career path

A machining career Sự nghiệp gia công

A standard career path combines four years of workplace apprenticeship (6,330 hours) with four levels of technical schooling (870 hours), culminating in an interprovincial Red Seal certification in Canada (or NIMS credentials in the US).

APPRENTICESHIP

6,330 h on the job

Paid workplace training accumulated across the program, from basic setups to complex multi-axis work.

TECHNICAL SCHOOL

870 h in class

Four levels of in-school training (Levels 1–4) covering theory, manual skills and CNC.

CERTIFICATION

Red Seal / NIMS

The interprovincial Red Seal exam (CA) or NIMS credentials (US) certify a journeyperson machinist.

02Cấu trúc chương trình · Program structure

Standard program structure Theo SkilledTradesBC / BCIT

In British Columbia, programs follow the SkilledTradesBC Harmonized Machinist Outline and are offered at institutions such as the BCIT Machinist Apprenticeship Program.

FOUNDATION

Foundation Program · 25 weeks

Optional entry point: shop safety, applied math, blueprint reading, and manual lathe/milling basics — granting Level 1 technical credit and 500 work-based hours without an employer sponsor first.

LEVEL 1 · 7 wk

Fundamentals

Workplace safety (WHMIS), hand tools, layout, metrology (micrometers/calipers), drill presses, power saws, and introductory manual lathes.

LEVEL 2 · 8 wk

Intermediate

Intermediate turning and milling, metallurgy, heat treatment, basic surface grinding, and introductory CNC concepts.

LEVEL 3 · 8 wk

Advanced

Advanced manual operations, precision cylindrical grinding, Geometric Dimensioning & Tolerancing (GD&T), and CNC programming/setup.

LEVEL 4 · 6 wk

Multi-axis & exam

Multi-axis CNC machining, CAD/CAM 2D/3D modeling, complex workholding, and Red Seal exam preparation.
